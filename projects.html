Customer Questionnaire Analysis Dashboard

A raw 166,000-row survey dataset, cleaned and reshaped into an analysis-ready star schema, with DAX measures for response rate, sentiment, and segment-level KPIs.

Click to expand ↓
Power BIDAXPower Query Data Cleaning166K RowsSurvey Analytics

Problem

A 166,000-row raw customer questionnaire CSV with no structure — nulls, inconsistent response categories, and no way for stakeholders to see patterns without manual filtering.

Approach

Applied full Power Query cleaning — removed nulls, standardised response categories, and reshaped the survey data into a star schema. Wrote custom DAX measures for response rate %, sentiment breakdowns, and segment-level KPIs across demographic splits.

Outcome

A single interactive report with drill-through capability, giving decision-makers one place to explore survey insights by segment instead of a static spreadsheet summary.

Revenue of 5 Countries — Sales Intelligence Report

Sourced from the same 166,000-row transactional dataset — a clean, benchmarkable view of revenue across five countries with time intelligence built in.

Click to expand ↓
Power BIDAXPower Query Time IntelligenceMulti-CountryRevenue Analysis

Problem

Multi-currency revenue figures and inconsistent country codes across a large transactional dataset made it impossible to compare performance across markets side by side.

Approach

Parsed multi-currency revenue columns and resolved country-code inconsistencies in Power Query, then built a clean date table for time intelligence. Created DAX measures for Total Revenue, MoM Growth %, Country-vs-Average variance, and YTD vs Prior Year comparisons.

Outcome

A report enabling side-by-side revenue benchmarking across 5 countries, with map visuals and dynamic slicers for leadership to explore trends without waiting on a new export.
